# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or declarative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `NNNN` (link the record)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: what is true today, what problem or pressure demands a
decision, and which constraints are non-negotiable. State facts, not
conclusions. A reader who disagrees with the decision should still recognize
this section as accurate.

## Decision

The choice, stated in one or two sentences, followed by the specifics that make
it actionable.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what obligations this creates.
Include the costs honestly — an ADR that lists only benefits is advocacy, not a
record.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was genuinely on the table and why it lost. Options rejected
here are the ones a future reader is most likely to propose again.
